# AI assistance and responsibility

OpenAI Codex (OpenAI; used during preparation in 2026; exact model versions were not retained) assisted with code development and inspection, numerical reproduction, literature organisation, preparation of plotting code, manuscript drafting, and language editing. The assistance was not limited to grammar correction.

Reported numerical results were calculated by executable Python code from the public source observations. Figures were rendered programmatically from those outputs. Publication details and numerical statements were checked against source records and reproducible outputs. These computational checks do not constitute independent human validation.

No AI tool is listed as an author. The human author is responsible for reviewing and verifying the text, references, code, figures, and interpretations and for the final content. This disclosure records assistance; it does not assert that a journal has accepted the work or that final human review has been completed.

Pain Medicine requires disclosure in the cover letter and the Methods or Acknowledgments. Its publisher additionally requests the tool/version where known, actual uses, and the author's role. The journal-specific manuscript disclosure should remain consistent with this record and be updated if further assistance is used.
