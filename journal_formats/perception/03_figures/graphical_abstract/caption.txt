Graphical abstract. Exploratory participant-held-out prediction in one public cohort. M0 includes stimulus intensity and temporal variables; M1 adds cue mean; M2 adds cue SD and skewness indicators. Values are equal-participant RMSE. All coefficients are refitted per model and fold. The mean-only model recovered nearly all the observed additive-model gain. SD/skewness comparisons changed RMSE by less than 0.01 points in either modality; this is not evidence of equivalence, causal attribution, absence of uncertainty effects, external validity, or clinical benefit. Descriptive conditional intervals and the interaction model are reported in the article and supplementary outputs. Author: Umut Şimşekçi; October 2026.
